# EXECUTIVE AMENDMENT — END OF CHAPTER ONE

Phase 26 is **FULLY APPROVED**. It is the most important report in ELITEFX's history — not
because it found alpha, but because it showed our central question had been *the wrong question*
from the start.

```text
Chapter One question:  "How is the market built?"
We now have evidenced answers:
  · event-specific representations    · operational geometry (survives OOS)
  · semantic interpretation           · ecology as a background layer
  · and: Prediction ≠ Explanation ≠ Decision
```

Knowing the market is **not** the same as knowing what to do. That gap is **decision theory**,
not market structure.

---

# THE DISCOVERY — THREE INDEPENDENT DIMENSIONS

Phase 26's scoreboard: **0/9** variables carried Selection Decision Value (OOS), despite good
prediction and explanation values. For decades quant practice assumed `Prediction → Decision`.
The data refuses that chain.

# PRINCIPLE 58 — Three Independent Dimensions (APPROVED)

```text
Prediction Value, Decision Value, and Explanation Value are independent dimensions
and shall never be treated as interchangeable.
```

# PRINCIPLE 59 — Representations Act Only Through Evidence (APPROVED)

```text
Representations shall influence decisions only through evidence, never directly.
```

# PRINCIPLE 60 — Decision Value Is Decision-Specific (APPROVED)

The Phase 26 metric tested only the **selection** decision. Selection is one member of a family
(abstain, reduce size, hedge, diversify, wait, exit, suspend).

```text
Decision Value is decision-specific; failure under one decision does not imply
failure under all decisions.
```

**Report wording corrected** (Chief): not "no Decision Value" but **"no evidence of Selection
Decision Value under the metric used (OOS ΔEV via train-EV>0 selection)."**

# PRINCIPLE 61 — Two Separate Sciences (APPROVED)

```text
Market research and decision theory are separate scientific domains and shall evolve independently.
```

# PRINCIPLE 62 — Stop Expanding Representations That Don't Change Decisions (APPROVED)

```text
A research program shall stop expanding market representations once additional representations
fail to change decisions.
```
